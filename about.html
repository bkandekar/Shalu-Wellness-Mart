Our Story

About Shalu Wellness Mart

Your everyday destination for health, wellness, and natural living.

Our Mission

Shalu Wellness Mart is an online shopping destination offering products across health, wellness, agriculture, personal care, and everyday living categories. We believe that caring for personal vitality and nurturing plants and soil belong together under one wholesome approach.

Who We Are

Managed by Shalu Srivastava from Sangli/Miraj, our store operates with a straightforward commitment: providing reliable botanical solutions, natural agricultural inputs, and home care items with honest details, clear pricing, and responsive customer communication.

What We Offer

Our carefully chosen categories are tailored to meet daily routine and agricultural needs:

  • Health Care: Everyday botanical formulations and herbal decoctions for balanced living.
  • Wellness & Nutrition: Wholesome seed blends, amla preserves, and nutrient-dense additions to regular diets.
  • Agriculture: Bio-enzyme solutions, pure cold-pressed neem oils, and soil conditioners for farm fields.
  • Beauty & Personal Care: Gentle artisanal soap bars and infused hair oils free from aggressive chemicals.
  • Home & Lifestyle: Plant-derived floor cleaners and pure camphor room fragrance blocks.
  • Gardening & Plant Care: Sieved organic vermicompost and marine seaweed foliar sprays for terrace and balcony plants.

Our Core Approach

We do not make overstated medical promises or exaggerated crop yield claims. Instead, we list accurate ingredient lists, manufacturer information, and usage guidelines so our customers can make well-informed decisions.

Whether you are picking an everyday wellness tonic for your household or selecting an eco-friendly spray for your crops, our team is directly available via call and WhatsApp to assist you.
