Shipping & Delivery Policy

Information regarding dispatch, shipping estimates, and delivery coverage.

1. Order Processing & Confirmation

All product orders submitted via our website catalogue are processed as order requests through WhatsApp communication. Since stock levels and regional delivery logistics vary, our store team reviews each request personally before confirming the order.

2. Shipping Locations

We service local and regional pin codes in Sangli, Miraj, and neighboring districts, as well as select pan-India courier routes for eligible dry items. Bulky agricultural tonics or liquid items may be subject to specific courier service constraints depending on the destination.

3. Delivery Charges

Delivery charges are calculated based on package weight, volume, and destination pincode. Delivery fees are shared and agreed with you on WhatsApp prior to dispatch. There are no surprise hidden delivery fees.

4. Estimated Delivery Timelines

Orders confirmed on working days are typically packed and handed to local courier or parcel services in a reasonable timeframe. Exact estimated delivery dates depend on destination distance and carrier transit times. You will receive tracking details or transport updates via WhatsApp upon handover.

5. Damaged or Tampered Parcels

If you receive an outer package that appears significantly torn or damaged during transit, please photograph the parcel before opening and notify us immediately via WhatsApp or phone so we can coordinate resolution with the delivery partner.
