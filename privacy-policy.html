Data Protection

Privacy Policy

How we respect and handle your personal contact and delivery information.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our website, we only collect the information you choose to provide in our checkout or contact forms: your name, telephone number, optional email address, and delivery destination details.

2. How Your Information Is Used

We use your contact and delivery information strictly for:

  • Formatting your WhatsApp order request so you can communicate directly with our store.
  • Arranging parcel packaging, dispatch, and delivery with logistics partners.
  • Answering your specific questions regarding product batches and directions of use.

3. We Do Not Collect or Store Financial Credentials

Our website never requests, processes, or stores your credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, net banking passwords, or UPI PINs. All payment arrangements are coordinated directly between you and the store management.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal telephone numbers or customer data to marketing lists or brokers. Address details are provided only to courier or delivery personnel solely to facilitate physical parcel delivery.

5. Your Choices & Data Updates

If you have previously placed an order and wish to update or delete your contact details from our records, please reach out directly to Shalu Srivastava via our store email or WhatsApp.
